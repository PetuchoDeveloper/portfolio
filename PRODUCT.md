# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary visitor: a small-business owner who needs a website customers can actually find. They are comparing local agencies, template builders, and freelancers. They decide quickly from the first screen.

Secondary visitor: a technical or institutional buyer who needs a production web application. They use the same site as proof of engineering discipline, not as the opening offer.

## Product Purpose

This is Petucho's freelance client-acquisition site. It must win website work from small businesses and rank in search. Success is a real email about a project, in English or Spanish.

## Positioning

Institutional engineering habits applied to a small-business website: written scope, weekly working previews, tests where they matter, security review included, full handover. A template builder cannot truthfully claim this. A typical agency often will not.

## Operating Context

Static Astro site, zero client JS by default, self-hosted fonts, no trackers, no backend. Contact is mailto-first. Preview is loopback-only over SSH until a real domain is set. Identity fields live in `src/data/site.ts`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Two offers: (1) small-business websites, (2) full-stack web apps.
- Security review is included in full-stack builds, not a standalone paid audit.
- Smart-contract study is credibility only.
- Handle is "Petucho". Location: Mexico. Languages: English and Spanish.
- Domain `petucho.dev` and email `hello@petucho.dev` are placeholders until the owner replaces them.
- Do not invent clients, testimonials, prices, or outcome metrics.

## Brand Commitments

- Redesign is a replacement visual world. The current dark violet look is anti-reference.
- Binding visual brief from the owner: light backgrounds from the cited references; black type; uncommon purple accents; bold, not timid.
- Binding references: recent.design items ovkkk8y, ivwtfzo, g5olomu, mk1ovg5, w9nr5j4.
- English site chrome; Spanish only for product copy when asked.

## Evidence on Hand

- Flagship written case study: SED (anonymous teacher-evaluation platform for a public Mexican university; Django + React; 1,750+ backend tests, 830+ frontend tests).
- Named but unpublished: video streaming stack, marketplace operations tool, automation verification gate. These stay "case study in progress".
- One blog post exists. No client testimonials. No public small-business website case study yet.
- Private SED portfolio reel exists off-site and is not a public claim.

## Product Principles

- Do not invent proof. Label synthetic demonstration material as synthetic.
- Small-business website work leads. Institutional work supports trust.
- The visitor should know what to do within seconds: start a project by email.
- Own the code, the domain, and the handover. No lock-in claims beyond what is true.
- Keep the site fast, searchable, and tracker-free.
